## The idea in one sentence

> A **group-buying (demand-aggregation) marketplace for home services, scoped to one residential society at a time**: neighbours pool the same job on the same day, the price falls publicly as flats join, and one vendor serves the whole building in a single trip.

## What the video does NOT answer (the gaps this plan fills)

1. Who creates a deal — only the secretary, or any resident? Who moderates?
2. What happens if the 20-flat threshold is **not** met by the deadline?
3. How is money taken — pay-on-join, pay-after-service, escrow, refunds on failure?
4. Where do vendors come from, how are they vetted, priced, and paid?
5. How does the vendor quote a slab price without being gamed?
6. What is the business model — take rate, vendor subscription, society fee, ads?
7. Why can't Urban Company / NoBroker / MyGate just ship this tomorrow? (moat)
8. What does the resident see **after** the service — warranty, complaints, ratings?
9. Cold-start: one society with 4 joiners and no vendor is a dead feed.
10. The whole non-feed surface: onboarding, verification of "I live here", notifications, payments, history, vendor app, society admin, ops console.

Answers to all ten are in `01-product-plan.md` onward.
